# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive,
professional, and considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, and conduct
that makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming.

Report concerns privately to [nimotecode@gmail.com](mailto:nimotecode@gmail.com).
Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation
when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
